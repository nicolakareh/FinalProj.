Our story

Four generations of produce people

People first, product second. But of course, it's a very close second.

It started with a fruit stand on West Broadway in South Boston in 1889. In 1973, Don Mignosa opened the first Fruit Center; Hingham followed in 1976 and Milton in 1979. Today his sons Mark and Michael run the two stores by the same rule their father did.
