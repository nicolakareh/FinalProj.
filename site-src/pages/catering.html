Good to know

Catering questions

Do you deliver?

Yes. Catering orders and gift baskets can be delivered or picked up in-store; choose the option when you order online or ask when you call. Everyday grocery delivery is handled through Instacart.

How far ahead should I order?

The sooner the better, especially around holidays and graduation season. Order online any time, or call the store to talk through timing for a large order.

Can you handle vegetarian guests?

Yes. Vegetarian sushi, salads, fruit and veggie platters, quiche, and cheese boards all cover the bases, and our kitchen can steer you toward the right mix.

Which store should I order from?

Whichever is closer to you. Milton is at 10 Bassett Street in East Milton Square, (617) 696-5274. Hingham is at 79 Water Street across from the harbor, (781) 749-7332.

Do you sell gift cards?

Fruit Center gift cards are available in any denomination at either store.
