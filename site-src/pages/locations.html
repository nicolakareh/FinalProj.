Frequently asked

Questions we get at the register

Do you deliver groceries?

Not directly, but both stores are on Instacart. Shop the aisles in the Instacart app or website and a shopper will pick and deliver your order the same day. Note that Instacart can't accept Fruit Center gift cards as payment.

Can I order ahead from the deli?

Yes. Build your deli order online at deli.fruitcentermarketplace.com and pick it up at the counter without waiting in line.

Do you offer catering?

We do: deli and sandwich platters, salads, fruit and veggie platters, entrées and sides, quiche, party favorites, sushi, wine and beer, and gift baskets, with in-store pickup or delivery. See the catering menu or call Milton at (617) 696-5274 or Hingham at (781) 749-7332.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Fruit Center gift cards are available in any denomination and can be purchased in-store at either location.

Should I bring my own bags?

Please do. Both Milton and Hingham have banned single-use plastic bags, so reusable bags are the way to go.

Can the Fruit Center support my fundraiser or team?

Every year we contribute to more than 100 local organizations. Send your request through the donation form on our main site at least 30 days before your deadline. Donation requests.

Are you hiring?

Often. The Fruit Center has been named one of The Boston Globe's Top Places to Work thirteen years running, and many of our people have been here for decades. See open positions.

What are your holiday hours?

Regular hours are 8 am to 7 pm every day. Holiday hours can differ, so give the store a call before you head over on a major holiday.

Still have a question? Call us.

A person answers the phone at both stores, 8 am to 7 pm, every day of the week.
