01Where it all started

Produce

The Mignosa family has been in the produce business since a fruit stand on West Broadway in 1889, and it shows. Our buyers have decades-long relationships at the Boston wholesale markets and are always looking for what's fresh, seasonal, and exceptional.

  • A wide selection of organic fruits and vegetables, plus organic packaged goods.
  • Local farms in season: June strawberries, August corn and tomatoes, October apples and cranberries.
  • Hard-to-find specialty items alongside the everyday staples.

  • Local in season
  • Specialty finds

Kinnealey Quality Meats

Our meat departments are run by Kinnealey Meats, a Boston family business since 1939 that also supplies some of the city's finest hotels and restaurants. The counter you see is a real butcher shop, not a case of shrink-wrap.

  • Fresh cuts prepared and packaged in-store, every day.

Since 2004, Rocky Neck Fish has owned and managed our fresh fish departments. Because they're both a seafood wholesaler and a retailer, they buy directly from fishermen, which means fresher fish and better prices at the counter.
