Synchronize medication program

Get all your medication on the same day every month. Medication synchronization, or med sync, lines up all of a patient's medications to be refilled on the same day.

How it works

Our specially trained staff and unique pharmacy software can synchronize all of a patient's medications to be ready on a single day of the month. This eliminates multiple trips to the pharmacy and prevents medication shortages.

Prior to each fill date, we call each patient to review their medication, identify any changes, answer questions, and counsel them on their medication therapy.

We can also coordinate whole families' refills for the same day.

  1. We line up your fills

    Our staff reviews everything you take and aligns the refill dates so they land on one day each month.

  2. We call you first

    Before each fill date a pharmacist calls to review your medications, note any changes and answer your questions.

  3. One pickup or delivery

    Everything is ready together. Pick it up, or have it delivered the same or next day.

Enroll in med sync.

Call us and we'll set up your synchronized fill date. No extra cost.
