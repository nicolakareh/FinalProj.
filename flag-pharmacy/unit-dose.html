Services/Unit dose packaging

Unit dose packaging

Blister cards that organize prescription medications into easy to use weekly or monthly cards.

The right medication, every day

Our unit dose packaging uses blister cards that organize prescription medications into easy to use weekly or monthly cards. These cards organize the medications to ensure that the correct medication is taken each day and offer a safe, convenient, and easier way to administer medicines to patients, thereby eliminating any possibility of medication errors.

Multi-dose packaging

Through our partnership with Dispill, each package contains a week's worth of medication individually blistered for a specific time and date. Each blister can be used as a medication cup and contains the entire patient's medication data printed right on it. The packaging is perforated, so if the patient is going out to lunch or away for the weekend, they can simply bring along the appropriate blisters.

Unit dose packaging is also available to health care providers and facilities.
