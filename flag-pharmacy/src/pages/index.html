About us

Established in 2006 to serve the greater Lynn community.

Flag Pharmacy was established in 2006 to provide pharmacy services to the underserved community of greater Lynn. We were first to offer drug monographs in over 13 languages, foreign language directions on the prescription labels, and have Russian and Spanish speaking pharmacists.

In 2010 we reopened Margolis Pharmacy, a staple in Chelsea for over 80 years, and we are dedicated to keeping this historical pharmacy open.
