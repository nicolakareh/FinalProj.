Home delivery

We offer delivery service to most locations north of Boston, often the same or next day. We can bring prescriptions to your home, workplace, or a preferred address.

Efficient home delivery and fast mail service

Flag Pharmacy offers efficient home delivery and fast mail service catering to your needs. Along with prescriptions, we deliver over-the-counter medication, first aid products and home health care equipment.

Flag Pharmacy is a contracted pharmacy provider for Greater Lynn Senior Services (GLSS) and is contracted to provide pharmacy delivery and medical equipment delivery to all GLSS patients in all the areas GLSS serves.

Don't have a paper prescription?

We will contact your health care provider. If you do have one, you can mail it to us or ask us to pick it up at your house.
