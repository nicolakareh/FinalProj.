Medication therapy management

Medication therapy management, also referred to as MTM, is a term used to describe a broad range of health care services provided by pharmacists, the medication experts on the health care team.

What MTM is

As defined in a consensus definition adopted by the pharmacy profession in 2004, medication therapy management is a service or group of services that optimize therapeutic outcomes for individual patients.

Our pharmacists provide medication therapy management to help patients get the best benefits from their medications by actively managing drug therapy and by identifying, preventing and resolving medication-related problems.

What to expect

  • A comprehensive review of all your prescriptions, over-the-counter medications and supplements
  • A personal medication list and action plan you can share with your doctors
  • Follow-up with your prescribers when a change is recommended
  • Counseling in English, Russian or Spanish
