New prescriptions

Three easy ways to get a new prescription filled at Flag Pharmacy.

  1. Bring it in

    Bring your paper prescription to the pharmacy and we will dispense it while you wait.

  2. Have your prescriber send it

    Your prescriber can send or call in the prescription directly to the pharmacy. Lynn: (781) 599-5900, fax (781) 599-5918. Chelsea: (617) 884-3524.

  3. Get it delivered

    If you don't have a paper prescription, we will contact your health care provider. If you do have one, you can mail it to us or ask us to pick it up at your house.

What to bring

  • Your prescription (or your prescriber's information so we can contact them)
  • Your insurance card. We accept most major insurance plans, including MassHealth and Medicare.
  • A list of the other medications and supplements you take, so our pharmacists can check for interactions
  • Your preferred language for directions and drug information. We print foreign language directions on prescription labels and offer drug monographs in over 13 languages.

No insurance?

We have the lowest cash pricing around and can help you find copay assistance programs. Just ask.
