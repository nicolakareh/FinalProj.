Keeping a Chelsea landmark open

Margolis Pharmacy had been a staple in Chelsea, MA for over 80 years. In 2010, Flag Pharmacy reopened Margolis Pharmacy and is dedicated to keeping this historical pharmacy open.

Margolis Pharmacy is a full service pharmacy with Spanish speaking pharmacist and staff. Patients get the same personal service, home delivery, medication management programs and competitive cash pricing as at Flag Pharmacy in Lynn.
