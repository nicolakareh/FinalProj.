About

A full-service pharmacy that specializes in personalized service, home health care and medication management.

Flag Pharmacy was established in 2006 to provide pharmacy services to the underserved community of greater Lynn.

Who we are

Flag Pharmacy is a full-service pharmacy that specializes in personalized service, home health care services, managed medications, refills and convenient packaging. We were first to offer drug monographs in over 13 languages, foreign language directions on the prescription labels, and have Russian and Spanish speaking pharmacists.

We have stores in Chelsea and Lynn, Massachusetts and offer delivery service to most locations north of Boston, often the same or next day. We accept most major insurance plans, including MassHealth and Medicare, and have the lowest cash pricing around.

What we offer

We offer only the highest quality FDA approved prescription medication, over-the-counter medication and first aid products, with efficient home delivery and fast mail service catering to your needs. Our store also carries home health care and durable medical equipment, dietary supplements, teas, herbals and tinctures.

Margolis Pharmacy

Margolis Pharmacy had been a staple in Chelsea, MA for over 80 years. In 2010, Flag Pharmacy reopened Margolis Pharmacy and is dedicated to keeping this historical pharmacy open. It is a full service pharmacy with Spanish speaking pharmacist and staff. Read more about Margolis Pharmacy.
