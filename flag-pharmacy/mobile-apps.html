Patients/Mobile apps

Your pharmacy, in your pocket.

Our easy-to-use mobile apps allow pharmacy customers to manage their entire family's prescriptions, order refills, set medication reminders and find pharmacy location information.

Free for Android and Apple devices

There is no charge to download or use the app.

  • Manage the whole familyKeep everyone's prescriptions in one place.
  • Order refillsRequest a refill in a couple of taps and choose pickup or delivery.
  • Medication remindersGet a reminder when it's time to take each dose.
  • Pharmacy informationHours, phone numbers and directions for both stores.
