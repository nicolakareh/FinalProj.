Transfer your prescriptions

Switching to Flag Pharmacy is simple. Tell us the name and phone number of your current pharmacy and the medications you take, and we will contact them to transfer everything for you. You'll get the same medications with the personal service, delivery and multilingual support we're known for.

  1. Call us or stop by with your current pharmacy's name and phone number.
  2. Share your insurance card and a list of your medications.
  3. We contact your pharmacy and your prescribers, and let you know when your prescriptions are ready.
