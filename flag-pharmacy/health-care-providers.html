Health care providers

A pharmacy partner for your patients.

Flag Pharmacy offers medication therapy management, home delivery, copay assistance programs, an adherence program, synchronized medication programs, compliance packaging, medication packaging and medication disposal services.

Working with prescribers and agencies

Send or call in prescriptions directly to the pharmacy. Lynn: (781) 599-5900, fax (781) 599-5918. Chelsea: (617) 884-3524.

Flag Pharmacy is a contracted pharmacy provider for Greater Lynn Senior Services (GLSS) and is contracted to provide pharmacy delivery and medical equipment delivery to all GLSS patients in all the areas GLSS serves.

Our pharmacists speak Russian and Spanish, and we print foreign language directions on prescription labels with drug monographs in over 13 languages, so your patients understand their therapy.

Services for your patients

What we provide

Medication therapy management

Comprehensive reviews that identify, prevent and resolve medication-related problems.

Home delivery

Same or next day to most locations north of Boston.

Copay assistance programs

We help patients enroll in manufacturer and foundation assistance.

Adherence program

Compliance beyond Medicare and insurer targets.

Synchronized medication

One fill date per month, with a pharmacist call before each fill.

Compliance & medication packaging

Unit dose blister cards and Dispill multi-dose packaging.

Medication disposal

Safe disposal of unused and expired medications.

Immunizations

COVID-19, RSV, flu, shingles, pneumonia and Tdap.
