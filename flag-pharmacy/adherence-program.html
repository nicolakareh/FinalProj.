Adherence program

Our adherence program helps patients achieve greater compliance goals than those set by Medicare and most insurances.

Compliance packaging

Flag Pharmacy offers both unit and multi-dose compliance packaging. We have partnered with Dispill to provide multi-dose compliance packaging.

Each package contains a week's worth of medication individually blistered for a specific time and date. The blisters are large enough to accommodate medication for patients with multiple disease states, and each blister contains the entire patient's medication data printed right on it.

Why it works

  • Reduces medication errors and mix-ups
  • Increases compliance
  • Gets the patient the correct dose at the correct time
  • Each blister can be used as a medication cup
  • The packaging is perforated, so if you are going out to lunch or away for the weekend you can simply bring along the appropriate blisters

Combined with our Synchronize Medication Program, all of your medications arrive together, packaged by dose, on a single day each month.
