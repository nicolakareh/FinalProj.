Drop finished files here with these exact names. The site checks for them at build time and swaps out the placeholder automatically; nothing else needs to change.

## Sourcing

- Your own footage of the aircraft is best. Real shots of your fleet will outperform anything generated.
- Licensed stock: search Pexels or Unsplash for "agricultural drone spraying", "crop spraying drone field", "agriculture drone aerial". Check the license allows commercial use.
- Generated: the prompts below were written for Higgsfield (GPT Image or Nano Banana, 16:9 or 3:2, 2K). Generation needs a paid Higgsfield plan.

Do not use manufacturer product photography from DJI or others; it is copyrighted.
