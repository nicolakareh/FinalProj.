How the produce gets here

Fresh is a daily decision

  1. Up before the sun

    Our buyers work the Boston wholesale markets every morning, with relationships that go back decades. Good produce goes to the people who show up.

  2. Local when it's ripe

    When New England farms are picking, we buy from them: berries in June, corn in August, apples and cranberries in the fall.

  3. Organic where it counts

    A wide selection of organic fruits and vegetables, plus organic packaged goods in the grocery aisles.

  4. Ask a produce person

    Not sure how to pick a melon or when the peaches will be ready? Ask. Someone on the floor has been doing this for twenty years.

Can't make it in? We'll come to you.
